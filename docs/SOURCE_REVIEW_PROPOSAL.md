# Historical source-verdict review

The operator reviewed four synthetic source cases: two violations and two
non-violations. These decisions were recorded for those exact observations in the
private experiment state. They are not general verdicts on arbitrary servers.

The original observation-specific records and approval references remain private.
Public fixture definitions in `fixtures/memory_cases.json` support reproducible
local tests; held-out expectations cannot be converted into human verdicts or
lesson approvals. The separate [lesson publication review](LESSON_PUBLICATION_REVIEW.md)
explains the private-publication boundary.
