# Private lesson publication review

lesson bodies for **private Senso publication**. That approval did not authorize
public redistribution of the bodies or provider identifiers.

The original immutable review record remains in private local evidence. Public
source and distributions include this notice instead of the lesson bodies,
private identifiers, exact approval digests or policy bindings. The library's
synthetic fixture definitions and tests document the mechanism without granting
any new publication approval.

The historical comparison used three active lessons after one revocation and
showed no measured decision improvement. See [RELEASE_RESULT.md](RELEASE_RESULT.md)
for the measured result and its limits. Consumers must review their own verdicts
and exact lesson content before any publication.
